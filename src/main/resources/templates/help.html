Hello

Register

To register, input a Username, email and password and press register



Wishlists

Once you are logged in, or have created a new user, you will be taken to the wishlist dashboard

Here you can see all of your wishlists, if you don't have any wishlists yet, you can create a new one by pressing "Create"



Create wishlist

Once you have pressed create wishlist from the dashboard you can input a desired name for the wishlist and press "Create"

And now you have a fresh new wishlist ready for wishes



Making wishes

From the dashboard, where you can see your wishlists, you can choose the desired wishlist by pressing the name of the wishlist you want to add wishes to

From here you will be taken to the specified wishlist, where you can press the large "+" sign

Now a new form will pop up, where you can input the name of the wish, the price and give it a link to the product you wish for.

Once the form is completed, you can press "Create" and if you regret you can always press "Cancel"

Now your new wish will appear before the large "+"



Modify wish

Once you have created a wish, you can press the "edit" button which takes you to a new form.

Here you can input the new name, price or link for the wish and press "Save Changes", and if you regret you can always press "Cancel"

Now your wish should be updated and appear with the new information



Delete wish

If you want to delete a wish, you can simply press the "Delete" button which appears as a small bin icon

Now your wish should be removed from the list of wishes.



Delete wishlist

If you want to delete an entire wishlist, simply navigate to the dashboard, which holds all of your wishlists and press the "Delete button" Which appears as a small bin icon



View Others wishlists

Once you are logged in, you have the option to view others wishlists

If they have sent you a link to their wishlist, simply input the link in the url, after you are logged in

Now the desired wishlist should appear and you should see the option to reserve a wish



Reserve a wish

If you are not able to see a wishlist, follow the help section "View Others wishlists"

Once you have navigated to the desired wishlist, you should see the option to "reserve" a wish on the wishlist

After pressing the "reserve" button the wish should now be reserved





Sustainable initiatives

We have implemented some proposed wishes for you to wish for a donation in your name as a wish

The proposed wishes are displayed under your current wishes in the wishlists
